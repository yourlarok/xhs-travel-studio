# Traveler voice synthesis

Use international reviews to understand what travelers notice, not to borrow sentences. Extract expectations, friction, perceived value, entrances, transfers, queues, menus, payment, language, suitability, and ordinary global-English vocabulary.

Synthesize across multiple reviews. Avoid platform catchphrases, reviewer-specific anecdotes, distinctive metaphors, and long excerpts. Write in the established calm local-editor voice. Useful framing includes “worth it if…”, “skip it when…”, and “choose the easier option if…”. Do not claim all foreign travelers share one preference.
