# Guide planning coordinates

## Duration

Use a range rather than false precision. Calculate from the traveler's practical start and end point when known. Include local transfers, expected queues, food, toilet and navigation pauses, plus a 15–25% buffer. Label overnight or intercity transport separately.

Useful bands:

- `1–2 hr`: one focused experience;
- `3–5 hr`: a half-day neighborhood guide;
- `6–9 hr`: a full day with breaks;
- `2–3 days`: a city introduction with one theme per half-day.

Do not fill a long itinerary merely to justify its duration. Jet lag, heat, crowds, children, accessibility, or luggage may require a lower-density route.

## Budget

Express budget as `¥min–max per person`. State whether it includes tickets, local transport, meals, and optional upgrades. Use current checked prices where they affect the range.

Useful planning bands, adjustable by city and year:

- `low`: mostly public space, public transport, and simple food;
- `medium`: paid attraction or activity, comfortable meal, occasional taxi;
- `high`: premium experience, guide, private transport, or destination dining.

Never convert to a foreign currency without a checked rate and date. RMB remains the primary figure.

## Execution difficulty

Score each friction factor from 0 to 2:

- booking: none / simple same-day / advance timed reservation;
- transport: direct / one transfer / several or rural connections;
- navigation: obvious / moderate walking / difficult-to-find entrances;
- language: English-friendly / translation helpful / Chinese interaction required;
- digital dependency: optional / one domestic app / several identity-dependent flows;
- physical load: light / sustained walking / stairs, altitude, heat, or long day.

Map the total to:

- `easy` (0–3): first-time visitor can execute with ordinary preparation;
- `moderate` (4–7): one or two meaningful friction points need preparation;
- `complex` (8–12): multiple failure points or specialist support may be sensible.

Always name the one factor most responsible for the rating. Do not call a plan easy solely because it is inexpensive.

## Plan variants

When useful, produce three genuinely different variants instead of stretching one route:

- **Easy entry:** shortest duration, low-to-medium budget, few dependencies.
- **Balanced day:** moderate duration and budget, one booking or signature experience.
- **Deep dive:** longer duration or higher budget, richer context, more execution friction.

Each variant needs its own promise and suitable traveler. Avoid changing only the price label while keeping the same itinerary.
