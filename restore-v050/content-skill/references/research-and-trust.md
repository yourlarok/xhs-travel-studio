# Research and trust

Prefer current official venue, transport, government, or booking documentation for operational facts. Use credible reporting or established travel services for context. Use traveler reports to identify friction and questions, then verify their factual claims elsewhere when possible.

Facts needing a checked date include visa and transit rules; opening days and hours; ticket prices and release times; passport or identity requirements; payment and foreign-card support; phone-number or app requirements; station exits and transport disruptions; seasonal access, closures, and special events.

Use one internal claim label: `official`, `observed`, `traveler_report`, `editorial`, or `unconfirmed`. Do not publish `unconfirmed` material as fact. Show readers the source or checked date when it affects whether a trip succeeds.

For web images, retain creator/source URL, license or permission basis, and download date. Search-engine availability is not permission. For AI images, keep model, prompt, creation date, and an internal AI-origin flag. Never label an AI scene as a photograph of a specific real venue.
