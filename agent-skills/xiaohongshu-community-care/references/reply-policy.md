# Reply policy

Answer the question first, add one useful caveat, and invite the smallest useful follow-up. Thank corrections and verify them. Do not promise current price, schedule, entry, passport or payment support without a checked date. Never reveal personal data. Avoid canned or sales-like replies.

Default action is `draft_only`. Sending requires an explicit click for that reply or a user-approved rule limited to low-risk categories. Log the exact final text and timestamp.
