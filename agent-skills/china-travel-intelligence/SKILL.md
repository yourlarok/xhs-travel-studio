# China Travel Intelligence

Build an evidence pack before writing. Use Chinese local content to discover operational detail and use international traveler reviews to understand questions, expectations, language, and failure points. Verify facts that could disrupt a trip with current authoritative sources.

## Separate source roles

- **Xiaohongshu:** discover entrances, station exits, route sequences, queues, local food, seasonal details, and repeated domestic-traveler observations.
- **Tripadvisor:** identify how international travelers describe value, confusion, comfort, expectations, accessibility, and whether an experience felt worth the effort.
- **Official sources:** verify prices, opening hours, reservations, transport schedules, passport support, closures, and rules.

Never present popularity as truth. Never infer foreign-passport support from a Chinese traveler report. Do not reproduce review sentences except a short attributed excerpt when licensing permits it.

Read [references/research-program.md](references/research-program.md) when building queries or selecting sample size. Read [references/evidence-schema.md](references/evidence-schema.md) when returning research for another agent.

## Produce decision-ready evidence

For each candidate route, calculate realistic door-to-door duration, per-person RMB budget, execution difficulty, the strongest traveler desire and anxiety, confirmed facts, contradictions, and unknowns.

Reject a topic when the sources are repetitive but do not reveal a meaningful traveler decision. Prefer an information gap with strong practical value over a merely high-interaction subject.
