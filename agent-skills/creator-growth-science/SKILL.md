# Creator Growth Science

Compare each post with the account's rolling median and posts of the same format, age, city, and objective. Analyze distribution, consumption, and intent. Mark missing metrics and lower confidence rather than inventing values.

Read [references/content-scoring.md](references/content-scoring.md) for scoring and [references/skill-evolution.md](references/skill-evolution.md) before proposing any Skill update.

Change one major variable at a time: promise, cover, title, card order, density, three-coordinate placement, visual system, CTA, or topic. Record the hypothesis before publishing and the evaluation window afterward.
