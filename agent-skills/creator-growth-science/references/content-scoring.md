# Content scoring

Score only when required metrics exist and normalize against comparable posts.

Image-text: qualified reach and CTR 20; swipe/completion 15; saves 20; meaningful comments 10; shares 10; follows/profile visits 10; information reliability and three-coordinate completeness 10; production/compliance 5.

Video: qualified reach and CTR 15; 3-second hold 15; average watch and completion 20; saves 15; meaningful comments 10; shares 10; follows/profile visits 10; production/compliance 5.

Return score, confidence, comparable set, contributions, and next test. Missing metrics are `not_scored`; do not redistribute points.
